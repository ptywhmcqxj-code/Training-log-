How to use these
Z1 Easy / Z2 Aerobic — Monday/Wednesday/Friday/Sunday runs. The most important zones because you spend 75–80% of training here. Talented juniors run too fast here — slow them down.

Z3 Tempo — first 5 weeks of base, Tuesday steady runs. Builds aerobic capacity without high CNS cost.

Z4 Threshold — Thursday threshold sessions, 8x1km work, combo sessions. The single most important pace for 1500m fitness.

Z5 VO2max — peak base Tuesday intervals (5x1000m), race-specific sessions. High stimulus, limit weekly volume here.

Z6 Race — 1500m race pace and faster. Saturday race-pace work in peak phase, kick development.
